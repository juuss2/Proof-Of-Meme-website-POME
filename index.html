About $POME

$POME is a pure meme token built on the simple idea that holding should be rewarding. No complicated staking, no points systems, no tiers — just hold, earn, meme, and repeat.

A 2% buy/sell fee powers the system. 30% of that fee is distributed to holders based on the percentage of supply they hold. The more you hold, the more you earn from the holder pool.

Built on Arc Network — a purpose-built Layer-1 designed as the Economic OS for the internet, with USDC as gas and sub-second finality. Launching through Piper, the meme launchpad that actually pays creators and holders.
